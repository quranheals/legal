Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026
Operator: Hamza Labib · Contact: quranheals.support@gmail.com

Quran Heals is a mobile app that helps you find verses of the Quran connected to how you feel, save the verses that matter to you, and write private reflections. Quran Heals is published and operated by Hamza Labib (“we”, “us”).

This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, who can read it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Quran Heals app and to the servers that support it.

In short

  • Anyone can use Quran Heals without an account. Without one, what you save stays on your device.
  • An account is optional and is only for people aged 16 or older who also meet any higher minimum age where they live. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store your account, favorites, preferences and encrypted reflections on our servers so they can sync between your devices.
  • The text of the reflections you sync is encrypted on your device with a key protected by your sync password before it is uploaded. We cannot read it, and we cannot recover it if you forget your sync password.
  • Not everything is end-to-end encrypted. We can read your account details, which verses you saved or reflected on (and when), your preferences, and any issue reports or emails you send us.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools in the app.
  • You can delete your account and synced data from the app at any time. If you can no longer use the app, you can ask us by email (section 10).

We collect information in three ways: from you, when you use the app, sign in, send an issue report or email us; from Apple or Google, when you choose to sign in (section 2); and automatically, when your device connects to our server (section 5). We do not obtain information about you from any other source.

1. Information stored on your device

Whether or not you have an account, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Reflections you write, in readable form. On your device they are protected by your device’s own security (for example, your passcode), not by an extra layer of app encryption. If the same reflection was changed on two of your devices before they synced, the other version may also be kept so you can review it.
  • Favorites (the verses you saved) and a short history of recently shown verses, used to avoid showing you the same verse again too soon.
  • Settings: app language, Quran translation display, appearance (light or dark), and Quran text size.
  • A cached copy of the list of feelings, so the app works with a weak connection.
  • If you are signed in: your basic account profile (an internal account number, whether you used Apple or Google, your email address if one was provided, and when the account was created); your sign-in tokens; and, after you enter your sync password, the key that decrypts your synced reflections. The tokens and the key are kept in your device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).

Signing out does not delete reflections or favorites from your device; they stay there, kept separately for that account. Uninstalling the app removes the information the app stored on that device.

2. Information we store if you sign in

Signing in is optional and only needed to sync between your devices. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

From your sign-in we keep only your provider’s identifier for your account, your email address (if the provider shares one) and whether the provider has verified it. With Apple, you can choose to share a private relay address instead of your real email. The Apple sign-in screen may ask whether to share your name, and Google’s sign-in may include profile details such as your name; our server does not receive your name from Apple and does not store any name or profile picture.

After you sign in, the app asks you to create a sync password (or enter the one you already set). This step is required to use an account.

3. Reflections, encryption, and your sync password

  • The first time you set up sync for your account, the app creates a random key on your device. Your reflections are encrypted with this key on your device before they are uploaded (using XChaCha20-Poly1305).
  • That key is itself encrypted on your device with a key derived from your sync password (using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). Only the encrypted key is uploaded. Your sync password and the unencrypted key never leave your device.
  • Decryption happens only on your devices, after you enter your sync password on each one.
  • Encryption covers the text of your reflections only. It does not cover your account details, favorites, preferences, issue reports, or which verses you reflected on and when.
  • Anyone who obtained the encrypted data could try to guess your sync password. A long, unique sync password makes this much harder.

We cannot recover your sync password or your synced reflections. If you forget your sync password, the app offers a reset. After you confirm your identity again with Apple or Google, the reset permanently deletes your encrypted reflections and encrypted key from our servers so you can set a new password. Reflections still stored on a device you use are kept on that device. Reflections that existed only on our servers cannot be recovered by anyone.

4. Issue reports and support emails

You can report a problem from inside the app, with or without an account. A report contains:

  • the category you choose and any comment you write;
  • an email address, only if you choose to enter one so we can reply;
  • context to help us find the problem: the verse and feeling on screen (if any), app language, translation display setting, app version, and platform (iOS, Android, or web).

Reports are not linked to your account and never include your reflections. Because they are not linked to your account, deleting your account does not delete them; email us to have a report deleted. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a comment. If you are under 16, please do not include your email address or other information about yourself in a report.

If you email us at quranheals.support@gmail.com, your message and our replies are kept in our support mailbox, which Google (Gmail) provides. Please never send us a password, a sign-in code, or the text of your reflections.

How long we keep reports and emails is set out in section 9.

5. Information processed automatically

  • Requests to our server. When the app loads verses or syncs, your device connects to our server, which runs on our hosting provider. Like any internet service, this involves your IP address and basic request details (such as the address requested and the time). Our server uses your IP address briefly in memory to limit abusive traffic (for at most one hour) and does not save it to our database. Our hosting provider (Render) keeps server logs, which can include IP addresses and request details, for 7 days.
  • Feelings you choose. When you choose a feeling, the app sends it to our server to fetch a matching verse, along with recently shown verses to avoid repeats. These requests are not sent with your account details, and we do not save them to our database.
  • Error monitoring. Our server sends technical error reports to an error-monitoring service (Sentry, in the United States). These reports are set up to leave out request contents, account identifiers, email addresses, tokens, and reflection data. The mobile app does not include crash reporting or analytics.
  • App updates. The app checks for updates through Expo’s update service, which receives technical information such as the app version, platform, and your IP address.
  • Links you open. Some links, such as “Read in Quran” (which opens tanzil.net), open in your browser. Those sites’ own privacy policies apply.

6. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • provide the app, including finding verses for the feeling you choose;
  • if you sign in, verify your identity with Apple or Google, keep you signed in, and sync your favorites, preferences, and encrypted reflections between your devices;
  • keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and find and fix errors;
  • delete your account and revoke Apple access when you ask, and keep a minimal record showing that we did (section 9);
  • answer support emails; and
  • read and reply to issue reports.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions, and we do not sell or rent it.

10. Deleting your information

You can delete your account in the app at any time from Settings → Account → Danger Zone → Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately. It permanently deletes from our database your account, sessions, favorites, preferences, encrypted reflections, encrypted reflection key, and Apple revocation token, and for Apple accounts it cancels Quran Heals’ access to your Apple ID. After deletion, we keep only the limited records described in section 9.

If you cannot use the app, you can ask us by email. We aim to acknowledge your request within 2 business days, confirm that you own the account with a one-time code sent to the email address stored on it, and complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving a valid request.

Deletion in the app also removes that account’s reflections and favorites from the device you use to delete it. Copies stored on your other devices stay on those devices until you uninstall the app there. Full details are on our Account & Data Deletion page.

You can also delete individual reflections and favorites in the app at any time.

11. Security

Connections between the app and our server use HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored on our server only as one-way hashes, and on your device in secure storage. The text of synced reflections is encrypted on your device (section 3). Resetting reflection sync, and deleting an account signed in with Google, require you to confirm your identity again with Apple or Google. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Please use a strong, unique sync password and keep your device protected.

If a confidentiality incident involving your personal information presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec, and take steps to reduce the risk, as the law requires.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under Quebec and Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • receive a copy of the information you gave us, in a structured, commonly used format;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by deleting your account or by not signing in; and
  • have it deleted.

You can:

  • use Quran Heals without an account, and send issue reports without an email address;
  • delete your account in the app at any time (section 10);
  • remove Quran Heals’ access in your Apple ID or Google Account settings (this does not by itself delete your Quran Heals data); and
  • email quranheals.support@gmail.com for any other request, including access to, correction or deletion of your information or an issue report.

How and when we respond

  • We aim to acknowledge your request within 2 business days.
  • Before acting, we may need to confirm that you own the account or information concerned. We will never ask for your Apple, Google, or sync password.
  • We respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request: one that tells us what you are asking for and gives us enough information to find the information concerned. Quebec law requires us to answer requests for access or correction within 30 days of receiving them.
  • If the law that applies to you sets a shorter deadline, we meet it. If the law allows us more time and we need it, we will tell you before the deadline, explain why, and say when you can expect our answer.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data-protection authority where you live. In Quebec, this is the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca); elsewhere in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

13. Age requirements and children

Quran Heals is available for reading Quran verses without an account. You must be at least 16 years old, and meet any higher applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction, to independently create or use a Quran Heals account.

Quebec law requires a parent’s consent before personal information is collected from a person under 14. Because accounts are only for people aged 16 or older, we do not create accounts for younger people and do not ask for a parent’s consent.

Before your first sign-in on a device, the app asks you to confirm that you meet the minimum age. This is a self-declaration: the app does not ask for your date of birth and does not verify your age or identity. Only the fact that you confirmed, and when, is stored, on your device.

We do not knowingly create or keep accounts for people under the required age. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under that age, we will delete it. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account or given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
