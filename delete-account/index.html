Account & Data Deletion

Quran Heals, by Hamza Labib · Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Quran Heals account and the data associated with it, in the app, from any device where you can sign in with the same Apple or Google account.

Two ways to delete your account:

  1. In the app: Settings → Account → Danger Zone → Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately.
  2. If you can no longer use the app: sign in on any phone and delete there, or email quranheals.support@gmail.com.

If you never signed in, there is no Quran Heals account. Everything you saved is on your device only, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Option A: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Quran Heals on a device where you are signed in.
  2. Open Settings from the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the Account section. Below it, in the Danger Zone, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, type the confirmation word shown (DELETE in English), and tap Delete my account.
  5. If your account uses Google, you will be asked to sign in with Google again to confirm it is you. If your account uses Apple, you may be asked to sign in with Apple again.
  6. When deletion is complete, the app shows a confirmation and you are signed out.

Deletion in the app normally completes within seconds. If something goes wrong (for example, no internet connection), nothing is deleted, you stay signed in, and you can try again.

What in-app deletion removes

From our servers, permanently and immediately:

  • your account (sign-in provider, provider identifier, and email address);
  • all sessions, which signs out every device using the account;
  • your synced favorites, including markers for removed favorites;
  • your synced preferences and settings, including the feelings you chose recently;
  • your encrypted reflections, including other saved versions and markers for deleted reflections;
  • your encrypted reflection key;
  • your synced Wirds and Khatmah records (My Reciting and Khatmas in the app), including markers for deleted ones, and their sync record; and
  • for Apple accounts, the stored Apple token. We also cancel Quran Heals’ access to your Apple ID with Apple before deleting anything else.

On the device you use to delete the account, the app also removes that account’s reflections, favorites, Wirds, Khatmah records and recently chosen feelings. Settings such as language and appearance stay on that device.

Option B: If you can no longer use the app

You do not need your old device. Install Quran Heals on any iPhone or Android phone, sign in with the same Apple or Google account, and delete your account in the app as described in Option A. Deletion takes effect immediately.

If you cannot do that, email us:

To: quranheals.support@gmail.com
Subject: Quran Heals account deletion request

Please include whether you signed in with Apple or Google, and the email address of that account. If you chose “Hide My Email” with Apple, include the private relay address if you know it (it ends in privaterelay.appleid.com and is listed in your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple).

Never send us a password. We will never ask for your Apple password, Google password, or Quran Heals sync password, or for any sign-in code.

Verifying a request sent by email

  • We must confirm that you own an account before deleting it. We never delete an account just because we received an email.
  • Our process for confirming ownership by email is being set up and is not available yet. Until it is, we will reply to your email, keep your request, and help you delete your account in the app. When email verification is available, we will contact you to complete your request, and we will update this page.

How long it takes

  • In the app: immediately.
  • By email: we aim to reply within 2 business days, and we will tell you what happens next. We answer privacy requests within the time the law that applies to you requires.

For Apple accounts, you can also cancel Quran Heals’ access yourself in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple). For Google accounts, you can remove access in your Google Account’s security settings. Removing access this way does not by itself delete your Quran Heals data.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • Reflections and favorites: delete or remove them individually in the app. Synced copies are removed from your other devices; a marker with only the verse reference and time is kept on our servers until you delete your account, so the item does not reappear.
  • All synced reflections: if you forget your sync password, the reset option deletes all your encrypted reflections and your encrypted key from our servers. Reflections stored on your device are kept there.
  • Issue reports: email us with the address you entered in the report, or describe it, and we will delete it.

What remains after account deletion, and for how long

Everything listed under “What in-app deletion removes” is deleted from our database immediately. Our database host, MongoDB Atlas, makes an automatic backup once a day and keeps the last 8 daily backups, so a copy of your data can remain in a backup for up to about 8 days after deletion, until that backup expires. The following are also kept separately, for limited periods:

  • Support emails. If you wrote to us, the email conversation about a deletion request is deleted 60 days after your request is completed. Other support conversations are deleted 12 months after the last message. We delete these from our support mailbox by hand. Google, which provides that mailbox, may keep residual copies for a limited time under its own policies.
  • Verification records. For email requests, we keep a record of the verification (an internal account number, the sign-in provider, a one-way coded form of the code and of the email address, and dates; never the code or the email itself). It is deleted 30 days after the code expired, or 60 days after your request is completed.
  • A minimal deletion record. For deletions we carry out on request, we keep a short record for 3 years, as evidence that we did it: the request reference, the date, the internal account number, the sign-in provider, the outcome, and how many records were deleted. It contains no email address, verification code, password, token or reflection content.
  • Issue reports. They are not linked to your account, so account deletion does not remove them. They are deleted 12 months after you sent them, or sooner if you ask us by email.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider (Render) keeps server logs, which can include IP addresses, for 7 days. Our error-monitoring service (Sentry) keeps technical error reports, which are set up to leave out account details, according to the retention settings of our Sentry service plan. The retention period may change if our service plan changes.
  • Copies on your devices. Reflections and favorites stay on any other device where you used the account, kept separately and no longer synced, until you uninstall the app on that device. Anything you saved while signed out, and settings such as language and appearance, stay on the device until you uninstall the app.
  • Apple and Google. Records kept by Apple or Google about your use of their sign-in services are governed by their own policies.

We keep any of these longer only when it is necessary for a legal obligation, a dispute, or a security investigation, and then only for that specific record, for a defined period that we review at least every 12 months.
